🏥 Smart Emergency Medical ID & QR Bracelet System

A healthcare-focused web application developed as a TÜBİTAK project to improve emergency response and provide quick access to critical patient information.

The system is designed for individuals who may become lost, experience medical emergencies, or require special assistance, including people with dementia, patients with medication allergies, and individuals with important medical conditions.

Users can create personal accounts and securely register their essential medical information, emergency contacts, and other relevant health details. This information is linked to a QR code integrated into a wearable identification bracelet, allowing authorized access to critical details during emergency situations.

### ✨ Key Features

- 🏥 **Personal Medical Profiles:** Users can create accounts and register essential medical information.
- 💊 **Medication & Allergy Information:** Store important medication details, allergies, and medical conditions that emergency responders should know.
- 🧠 **Support for Vulnerable Individuals:** Designed to assist people with dementia, individuals at risk of getting lost, and patients who may need special attention.
- 📱 **QR Code Identification:** Each user can access their medical identification through a QR code attached to their wearable bracelet.
- 🚨 **Emergency Information Access:** Quickly display essential patient information to help emergency responders make informed decisions.
- 📞 **One-Touch Emergency Calling:** Provide quick access to emergency calling and registered first-degree relatives.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Emergency Contact Management:** Store and access the contact information of designated family members.
- 🌐 **Web-Based Platform:** Manage personal medical information through an accessible web application.
- ⚡ **Emergency-Focused Interface:** Designed to make important information accessible with minimal steps during critical situations.

### 🔄 How It Works

1. Users create an account through the web application.
2. They enter their medical information, medication allergies, relevant health conditions, and emergency contact details.
3. A QR code is associated with their personal medical profile and wearable identification bracelet.
4. In an emergency, a person can scan the QR code using a compatible device.
5. Essential medical information and emergency contact details become accessible to help guide the initial response.
6. Emergency calling options allow quick communication with emergency services or registered relatives.

### 🎯 Project Goal

The primary goal of this TÜBİTAK project is to improve emergency identification, reduce delays in accessing critical medical information, and support individuals who may have difficulty communicating their medical needs.

By combining wearable QR code technology with a centralized medical information platform, the project aims to provide a practical solution for emergency preparedness, patient identification, and rapid access to essential health information.
